# Prompt template for the worker

Seven sections, fixed order. Section 4 — conditional scheme only. The prompt is self-contained: the worker does not see the analysis.

## What goes into each section

1. **Goal and outcome** — the goal; what should become possible or happen after the result; the chosen scheme (baseline / ambitious right away / conditional) and bar. For a new or complex task the ambitious bar is phrased as a learning goal, its number a hypothesis.
2. **Mandatory requirements and boundaries** — verbatim from the original task. Contradictions between requirements — on a separate line, no silent resolution.
3. **Materials and access** — two lists: required and optional. The line: "If required ones are missing — ask me before starting work."
4. **Gate check** — five attributes from `core/gate.md`, three outcomes with actions, the ordering rule.
5. **Approach and stages** — only accepted improvements; question → method → verifiable result; what is handed over.
6. **Acceptance criteria and verification** — criteria measure the goal, not a proxy; current level or reference; independent verification method; falsification test; what counts as gaming the metric.
7. **Completion and report** — completion conditions; report format per `core/report-format.md`; requirement to list assumptions, unverified conclusions, and blockers.
